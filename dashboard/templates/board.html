Insight 1 — demand through the day

PySpark aggregate over the full history in HDFS. Amber bars are rush hours; the line is mean trip duration on the right axis.

Two commuter peaks. Duration rises to ~50 min in rush hours against ~41 elsewhere — the 1.2× congestion effect the model learns.

Trip mix by traffic

Share of all trips by observed traffic level.

Insight 2 — what congestion costs

Minutes per kilometre, normalised for trip length so this isolates traffic rather than distance.

Gridlock more than doubles travel time per km against light traffic. This is the evidence for using traffic as a model feature.

Insight 3 — demand by zone

Trips and fares per pickup zone, computed by PySpark from HDFS.

Near-uniform across zones, so dispatch gains come from timing rather than geography.
